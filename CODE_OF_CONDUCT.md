# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming
and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Participants should demonstrate respect, kindness,
professionalism, and constructive communication.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, and inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and
enforcing acceptable behavior standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces
and community interactions.

## Enforcement
Violations may be reported privately to the project
maintainers through the repository's contact channels.
Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers may take actions including:

1. Correction
2. Warning
3. Temporary Ban
4. Permanent Ban

Actions depend on the severity and frequency of violations.

## Attribution
This Code of Conduct is informed by the principles
of the Contributor Covenant.
